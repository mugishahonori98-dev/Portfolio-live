IoT Smart Attendance System

Manual attendance can be slow, difficult to review, and vulnerable to recording errors. This learning concept proposes an RFID card reader and an Arduino or compatible microcontroller, followed by a planned backend or data store and a future attendance dashboard. Student lookup, timestamps, attendance status, duplicate scan prevention, summaries, and export remain planned.
