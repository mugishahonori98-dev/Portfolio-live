IoT Smart Attendance System

Problem: Manual attendance can be slow, difficult to review, and vulnerable to recording errors.

Proposed solution: An RFID reader would scan a student card, an Arduino or compatible microcontroller would process it, and a future dashboard would record the attendance.

Technology ideas:

RFIDArduinoSensorsDjangoSQLiteDashboard

Planned features: Student identification, timestamp, attendance status, duplicate scan prevention, student record lookup, dashboard summary, and future export.
